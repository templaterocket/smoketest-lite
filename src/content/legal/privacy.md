## What we collect

**When you sign up or send a form**, we collect what you type: your email address and, depending on the form, your name, a message and your answers to a few questions about your needs. With it we receive the page you signed up on, the address of the page that sent you here, and any campaign tags in the link you followed (such as `utm_source`). The form sends this straight from your browser to the form service listed under "Who handles your data"; the site itself stores nothing.

**Visit statistics**, if this site uses them, are listed under "Who handles your data". They do not identify you.

## Why we use it and on what basis

- **To send you what you signed up for** — the launch news or a reply to your message. Basis: your consent, given when you submit the form. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in our emails or by writing to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
- **To understand which channels people come from** — counting signups by source. Basis: our legitimate interest in deciding what to build, using as little data as possible.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.
